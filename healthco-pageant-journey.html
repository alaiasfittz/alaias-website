01
Goal One

Shift how young women
view health & dieting.

Moving the conversation from shame and restriction to ownership, nourishment, and balance — because I lived through the confusion of mixed messaging on my own 75+ lb journey.

  • Partner with organizations like NEDA and universities
  • Teach simple, sustainable habits women can actually maintain
  • Champion a new standard for women's health in Florida
  • Because when women have the right tools, they change their lives
02
Goal Two · Signature Program

Healthy, Anywhere.
No matter the zip code.

A statewide initiative making healthy living accessible to every woman — through practical, low-cost tools whether she's shopping at a grocery store, dollar store, or gas station.

  • 30–45 min workshops: "Healthy Eating on $50/Week"
  • Live demos: build a meal, show real food swaps
  • Partner with colleges, high schools, community centers
  • Digital amplifier: YouTube, Reels, TikTok challenges
  • Free downloadable resource hub for every woman

through social media.

  • Reach a new generation of women authentically
  • Highlight real impact this organization is making
  • When young women see themselves in it, they believe they belong
  • Representation isn't just being seen — it's making others believe they can step in too
