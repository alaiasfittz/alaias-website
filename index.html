Who Is Alaias Fittz?

Creator.
CEO. Competitor.

Alaias Fittz is the founder and CEO of The Health Co., a media and production company on a mission to reach 100 million women and change how they think about themselves, their health, and what's possible.

Her story begins in Cleveland: overweight, deeply insecure, and surrounded by a family environment marked by addiction and loss. Determined to create a different future, she lost more than 75 pounds, earned a Division I Track & Field scholarship to Michigan State University, and graduated with a double major in Accounting and Marketing.

After college, she joined EA Sports, where she created and led Field the Future, a brand marketing program that impacted more than 1,000 students annually and provided over $100,000 in scholarships for students pursuing STEM degrees. Alaias successfully pitched and secured support from organizations including Nike, Microsoft, and the NFL to help bring the vision to life.

Today, her work has generated more than 30 million YouTube views, supports a growing team of six, serves executive consulting clients, and continues to expand through her platform as Miss Lake Nona USA, competing at Miss Florida USA 2026.

She isn't just a content creator. She's proof that the story you tell yourself determines the life you build.

Her breakthrough came when she stopped focusing on becoming smaller and started focusing on becoming healthier. By building simple, sustainable habits and changing the way she thought about fitness, she transformed her life and maintained that transformation for more than six years.
