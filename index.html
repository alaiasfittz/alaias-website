Why This Work Matters

The data is clear.
Women are still not okay.

My story started as one girl's struggle with her body and her confidence. But the data shows it was never just my story — it's a global crisis. This is why I do what I do.

of women globally considered themselves beautiful in 2004.
Dove spent 20 years and billions of dollars trying to fix that. In 2024, women feel less confident than they did in 2016. It's getting worse, not better.

Who Is Alaias Fittz?

Creator.
CEO. Competitor.

Alaias Fittz is the founder and CEO of The Health Co., a media and production company on a mission to reach 100 million women — changing how they think about themselves, their health, and what's possible.

Her story starts in Cleveland, Ohio — overweight, deeply insecure, and surrounded by a family environment marked by addiction and loss. She lost 75+ pounds, earned a Division I Track & Field scholarship to Michigan State University, graduated with a double major, and went on to create "Field the Future" at EA Sports — impacting 1,000+ youth annually through Nike, Microsoft, and the NFL.

Today: 26M+ YouTube views, a team of six, executive consulting clients, and competing as Miss Lake Nona in Miss Florida USA 2026.

She isn't just a content creator. She's proof that the story you tell yourself determines the life you build.
