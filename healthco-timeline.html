01
Chapter One
Cleveland,
Ohio

The Early Years.

Growing up in Cleveland, Ohio wasn't easy. Alaias was overweight, deeply insecure, and navigating an identity that didn't fit neatly into the world around her — and a home environment that required her to grow up fast.

Grew up in a predominantly white neighborhood as one of few Black girls in school — identity, belonging, and self-worth were daily struggles
Over 200 lbs as a teenager — deep insecurity, body image challenges, sense of not belonging
Family environment marked by substance abuse across multiple family members
Parents met in recovery
Lost both of mother's brothers to overdose
Brother began using drugs during adolescence
Close family friend also lost to drug abuse
Despite the chaos — channeled pain into discipline and purpose. The foundation was being built, even when it didn't feel like it.

Chapter Two
High
School

The Transformation.

This is where everything changed. Not just physically — but at the identity level. Alaias didn't just lose weight. She rebuilt who she was and what she believed she was capable of.

Lost 75+ pounds — a physical AND identity-level transformation that became the foundation of her entire mission
Fell in love with fitness as a vehicle for confidence, not just weight loss — this distinction became the core of The Health Co.
Graduated with a 4.2 GPA and high honors
Earned a Division I Track & Field scholarship to Michigan State University
Earned multiple awards in the DECA Program — her first real introduction to marketing and business strategy

College was where Alaias proved she could do everything at once — compete at the D1 level, excel academically, intern at the world's best companies, and start building what would become The Health Co.

D1 Track & Field athlete at MSU — competing at the highest collegiate level while maintaining academic excellence

Deepened faith as an anchoring foundation through the pressure of athletic and academic demands

Chapter Five
Building
in Parallel

Two full-time jobs.

For four years, Alaias ran EA Sports and The Health Co. simultaneously — two full-time roles, fully self-funded, building a team and a brand from nothing while holding down a corporate career.

Grew YouTube to 26M+ views and 130K+ subscribers while employed full-time at EA Sports
Founded Fit Media — a boutique social media consulting practice serving C-suite executives and corporate leaders
Retained as personal brand strategist for Kat Cole, CEO of AG1 — a multi-billion dollar global health brand
Grew The Health Co. from solo operation to a team of 6, including a full creative and production department
Secured and managed brand partnerships with national health and wellness companies across UGC and integrated content campaigns
Architected a full content system and long-term programming strategy to scale toward 10M+ women across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok

"Success cannot come at the expense of your health. I collapsed from chronic stress and dehydration. That was the reckoning."

August 2025 — left EA Sports to go all in on The Health Co.

Alaias
Fittz.

Alaias Fittz is the founder and CEO of The Health Co., a media and production company on a mission to reach 100 million women — changing how they think about themselves, their health, and what's possible.

Her story starts in Cleveland, Ohio — overweight, deeply insecure, and surrounded by a family environment marked by addiction and loss. She lost 75+ pounds, earned a Division I Track & Field scholarship to Michigan State University, graduated with a double major, and went on to create "Field the Future" at EA Sports — a first-of-its-kind brand marketing program that impacted 1,000+ youth annually through partnerships with Nike, Microsoft, and the NFL.

After four years of building The Health Co. alongside her corporate career, Alaias made the leap. Today, her platform has generated 26M+ YouTube views, she leads a team of six, consults for executives at billion-dollar companies, and is competing as Miss Lake Nona in Miss Florida USA 2026.

She isn't just a content creator. She's proof that the story you tell yourself determines the life you build.
