Core Belief

"When you change the way you think,
you change your life. And you change your life
by doing the hard things, daily."

This isn't just a tagline — it's backed by neuroscience. When you do hard things, your brain releases dopamine and builds new neural pathways that make confidence and discipline easier over time. Our content is designed to make women experience this, not just hear about it.

Why This Work Matters

The data is clear.
Women are still not okay.

My story started as one girl's struggle with her body and her confidence. But the data shows it was never just my story — it's a global crisis. This is why I do what I do.

2%
of women globally considered themselves beautiful in 2004.
Dove spent 20 years and billions of dollars trying to fix that. In 2024, women feel less confident than they did in 2016. It's getting worse, not better.
Dove Real State of Beauty Report, 2024 · PR Newswire · Harvard University
52%
happier when women exercise regularly. 50% more energized. 80% less frustrated.
And still — over half of women globally have stopped exercising completely. 74% cite time pressure. 44% say intimidating environments. 42% don't feel fit or sporty enough.
ASICS Global Gender Exercise Study, 2024 · 25,000 Women · 40 Countries
1%
